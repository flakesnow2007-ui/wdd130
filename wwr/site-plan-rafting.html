Overview

Purpose

This website will enable us to reach those nature lovers across the world and bring them to our rapids. Letting the world know we respect nature will help gather our audience. It will also be a place they can learn about our team and goals so there is no question of our loyalty to nature.

Audience

We want to gather nature lovers, those who want to be in nature but not ruin it. Family friendly, is also our goal. We have no hidden agendas but to be a nature loving place. A getaway "cabin" for people all around the world. Got to make it onto trivagos page.
